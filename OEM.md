# Embed the runtime under your own brand

Use the Apache-2.0 runtime when your platform should expose API drift detection
and reviewed selective repair as its own feature. Configure statistical windows
to detect; installation alone supplies no evidence. Repair remains opt-in and
requires the supported reviewed rules and independent verification.

Build and test the exported runtime using [BUILD.md](BUILD.md), then wrap the
SDK in your platform's integration. Keep the original package/protocol identifiers
in interoperable calls. Renaming a product does not require renaming audit fields,
cryptographic domains, headers or npm/PyPI imports. No end-user badge or
AGINDOW-branded UI is required. You may use your own product name and domain.

Redistribute [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [NOTICE](NOTICE), preserve required source
notices, and identify modifications as required by Apache-2.0. Attribution in
these files is distinct from product branding. The license grants no trademark
endorsement or right to use AGINDOW logos.

There is no automatic drift contribution requirement or runtime royalty.
Your platform owns upstream credentials, evidence selection, local snapshots,
review, delivery and operational qualification. SDK faults retain the existing
availability behavior; OEM packaging adds no remote dependency to guard calls.

The hosted/private registry, console, shared corpus and operations are separate
works and are not included in this Apache export. Self-hosting the private OEM
registry requires a separate agreement and private source access. Its commercial
terms and production support require approval; the local composition is not a
hosted service offer. This runtime continues to work without that registry.
